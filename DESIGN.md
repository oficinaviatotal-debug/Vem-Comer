# Vem Comer Design Intelligence

## Source of truth
The interface is original to Vem Comer. Public design systems are analyzed for principles, not copied.

## Tokens
Maintain semantic tokens for color, typography, spacing, radius, elevation, motion, focus and status, with light/dark behavior where applicable.

## Components
Catalog, product card, menu, QR, table, order, kitchen, stock, cost, margin, payment, dashboard, chat and voice controls must expose clear operational states.

## Information forms
Choose the representation that best fits the task: process, SOP, checklist, table, timeline, workflow, decision tree, dashboard, journey map or before/after.

## Accessibility
Touch targets, contrast, keyboard/focus behavior, text scaling, reduced motion and screen-reader semantics are first-class requirements.

## Responsive intelligence
Adapt layout, density and interaction to phone, tablet, desktop, browser, connectivity quality and input modality.

## AI design agent
Given text, image, URL, document or screen reference, extract reusable design principles and generate original components consistent with this system.
