# Vem Comer — Benchmark Intelligence Synthesis

## Consolidated benchmark principles
1. AI storyboard/video pipeline for automatic content production.
2. Intent-specific landing pages and offers tied directly to the searched product or need.
3. Automated campaigns plus interactive commercial dashboard.
4. AI-readable design intelligence using structured design rules and tokens.
5. Public/paid design references are inspiration inputs, not copying targets.
6. Organizational second brain with shared decisions, state, learning and provenance.
7. Event-driven agent teams with decision, coordination, execution and approval roles.
8. Build-for-the-process: create the operational tool around the restaurant's real workflow.
9. Operations intelligence: priorities, delegation, automation, root cause, process, risk, capacity, bottleneck, KPI, feedback and before/after.
10. Partner distribution and attribution.
11. Workforce/point-of-service style compliance concepts where applicable: evidence, permissions, auditability and lawful data use.
12. Universal multimodal operation: voice, text, photo, document, spreadsheet, API and structured data.

## Product rule
Do not copy a benchmark. Extract the underlying capability and build an original Vem Comer implementation.
